How it works

Three steps. Each side. Done.

For renters

Need equipment

  1. 01

    Search nearby

    Pick a category (tractor, harvester, sprayer…) and we surface verified owners in your taluka.

  2. 02

    Book + pay deposit

    Choose dates, an operator if you need one, and pay a small deposit on UPI. Owner accepts.

  3. 03

    Use, rate, done

    Equipment shows up. You use it. Pay the balance. Rate the experience for the next farmer.

For owners

Have equipment

  1. 01

    List in 5 minutes

    Snap photos, name your rate, mark your availability. Brand + HP + inclusions in one screen.

  2. 02

    Accept requests

    Get notified, see the renter's KYC + rating, accept or skip. Calendar auto-blocks accepted dates.

  3. 03

    Earn 100%

    Money lands directly in your bank. We don't touch it. Ever.
